# Meridian dense QA policies — fictional and non-operative

Every entry is a fictional benchmark policy, not an operative offer or instruction for real orders. Prices use USD. Working days exclude weekends and the local public holidays. Source-stated restrictions remain attached to the affected policy.

## Shipping

- Standard stocked-item lead time: 3 working days; Scope: in-stock mainland orders only; Exception: remote islands require a separate quote.
- Free-delivery threshold: USD 500; Below-threshold delivery fee: USD 12; Scope: mainland orders only.
- Order cutoff: 14:00 local time; Consequence: orders accepted after the cutoff enter processing on the next working day.
- Tracking notification: emailed after dispatch; Qualification: a label-created notification does not prove carrier collection.
- Hazardous goods: unavailable through standard delivery; Requirement: do not apply the ordinary lead time to hazardous goods.

## Billing

- Standard invoice term: 30 calendar days; Scope: approved business accounts only; Exception: first-time customers prepay.
- Accepted payment methods: bank transfer and corporate card; Card surcharge: none; Qualification: personal cards are not supported.
- Currency: USD; Tax handling: listed prices exclude tax; Requirement: tax is calculated for the delivery jurisdiction.
- Disputed invoice notification: within 10 calendar days of invoice date; Requirement: include invoice number and disputed line items.
